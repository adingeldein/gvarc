title: "GVARC Repeater Requested for October 17 Community Event"
layout: news_item
author: N5MCL
---

The **New Braunfels Community Emergency Response Team (NB CERT)** will provide support services for the city's **Día de los Muertos celebration on Saturday, October 17, 2026**.

NB CERT has requested use of the **GVARC repeater from 9:00 a.m. to 9:00 p.m.** that day. Club members should be aware of the requested operating window when planning repeater activity.

For additional information, please contact **Skip, W5UTW**.
