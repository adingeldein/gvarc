The Guadalupe Valley Amateur Radio Club will gather for its annual **Christmas Party on Saturday, December 19, 2026**, at **Granzin's BBQ**. As in years past, it's an opportunity to enjoy good food and fellowship with fellow club members, friends, and family.

## When and Where

- **Date:** Saturday, December 19, 2026
- **Time:** 5:30–8:00 p.m.
- **Location:** Granzin's BBQ, private room

We'll begin gathering around **5:30 p.m.** and plan to go through the serving line at approximately **6:00 p.m.** We’ll wrap up around **8:00 p.m.**

## Everyone Is Welcome

**No reservations are required**, and friends and family are always welcome. Come spend an evening visiting with the GVARC community and celebrating the holiday season together.

For event details, see the [GVARC Groups.io calendar announcement](https://groups.io/g/gvarc/viewevent?eventid=3252966).
